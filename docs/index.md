## Why Chronobound exists

A mage has a few chores that never go away. Conjure food and water before you run out, keep Arcane Intellect and your armor up, buff the group, and carry runes for your teleports and portals. None of it is hard, but it is easy to forget in the middle of a fight or a long quest chain.

Chronobound keeps those chores in one place. A card shows up when something needs doing, and a click on the card does it. When you press Escape, your teleports and portals are right beside the game menu.

## What it does

- **Food and water.** A card when your conjured food or water runs low. Click it to conjure more, always your best rank. It also tells you when you have learned a better rank. [More](features/food-and-water.html)
- **Buffs.** A card when Arcane Intellect or your armor is missing or about to run out. Click it to cast. [More](features/buffs.html)
- **Buff key.** One key that casts what the cards ask for, then Arcane Intellect on the next party member who is missing it. [More](features/buff-key.html)
- **Travel panel.** Press Escape: every teleport and portal for your faction, your rune counts and your Hearthstone, beside the game menu. [More](features/travel-panel.html)
- **Well-Rested and Well Fed.** A warning and a countdown before they run out. [More](features/well-rested-and-well-fed.html)
- **Explanations.** Hover over a card or button to read what it is and why it matters. [More](features/explanations.html)
- **Your layout and your look.** Move and resize every frame, and pick one of three skins. [More](features/quality-of-life.html)

Chronobound only loads on mages.

## The Bound family

Chronobound is one of the Bound addons, built on the same core as Lightbound (paladin). Each one looks like its class, but they work the same way: the same options window, the same cards, the same commands.
