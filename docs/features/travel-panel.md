# Travel panel

Press Escape, and beside the game menu you get every teleport and portal for your faction, your rune counts and your Hearthstone. Click one to cast it.

## What it does

- A panel titled **Travel** opens to the right of the game menu whenever the menu opens.
- Two columns: **TELEPORT** and **PORTAL**, one row per capital city of your faction.
  - Horde: Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff.
  - Alliance: Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus.
- **Click a teleport or portal you know to cast it.** The game menu closes after the click.
- Teleports and portals you haven't learned yet are grayed out, with the level under the name. Hover over one to see where you learn it.

  | | Teleport | Portal |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Orgrimmar, Undercity, Stormwind, Ironforge | level 20 | level 40 |
  | Thunder Bluff, Darnassus | level 30 | level 50 |

- Below the spells: `Runes: Teleportation 12 · Portals 4`, the number of Runes of Teleportation and Runes of Portals in your bags. A count below 3 is highlighted.
- At the bottom: **Hearthstone**, with the place it is set to. Click it to use it. The row only shows when the Hearthstone is in your bags.

## How to use it

1. Press Escape.
2. Click the city you want, in the **TELEPORT** column for yourself or the **PORTAL** column for your group.
3. Stock up on runes at a reagent vendor in a big city when the counts get low.

## Settings

In `/cb`, page **Travel**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show the travel panel when the game menu opens | On | Turns the panel on or off. |

## Good to know

**The panel doesn't show in combat.**
Spells can't be cast from it in combat, so it hides when a fight starts and stays away until the fight is over.

**A teleport needs a Rune of Teleportation, a portal a Rune of Portals.**
Reagent vendors in the big cities sell both.

**The panel can't be moved.** It always sits to the right of the game menu.

**Hover over a row** for what a teleport, portal or the Hearthstone does, when [explanations](explanations.html) are on.
