# Skins

Chronobound comes in three looks. They all work the same way, only the frames look different, so you can match the rest of your UI.

## The three skins

| Skin | Looks like | Good with |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Frost crystal** (default) | Ice-blue frosted glass with crystal shards in the corners, a faint mage crest behind each card, and a cold white rim that breathes slowly while a card needs you. Pale cyan titles. | The default UI, or if you just like the mage look. |
| **Blizzard** | The game's own gray and silver tooltips and dialogs, white text, gold titles, the classic buttons and check boxes. No crest. | A mostly default UI where ice blue would stand out. |
| **Flat** | Thin black edges on dark gray, Arial Narrow text. Ice blue stays as the one accent. No crest. | EllesmereUI, ElvUI and other flat UI packs. |

## How to switch

1. Type `/cb` and open the **Skin** page.
2. Click the **Skin** button to step through the skins (right-click goes back).
3. Click **Reload now**, or type `/reload` later. The new skin shows after the reload.

The skin is saved with your other Chronobound settings.

## What changes, and what doesn't

A skin changes the frames: colors, borders, fonts, buttons and check boxes in the options window, the cards and the travel panel.

In every skin, a status or count that needs you, such as `No water left` or a low rune count, stands out in a warm warning color.

## Things to know

**Why does it need a reload?**
Chronobound builds its frames once, when you log in. Switching in the middle of a session would leave some frames in the old look and some in the new one, so the switch waits for the next reload.

**Does Flat take its colors from EllesmereUI or ElvUI?**
No. Flat uses its own dark gray and Chronobound's ice blue.
