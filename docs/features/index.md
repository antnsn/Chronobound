# Features

Chronobound does the small mage jobs for you. Each page below covers one of them: what it does, how to use it, the settings in the `/cb` window, and the things that surprise people. New to the addon? Start with [Food and water](food-and-water.html) and [Buffs](buffs.html). Those cards are what you see most.

- [Food and water](food-and-water.html): a card when your conjured food or water runs low, or when you can make a better rank.
- [Buffs](buffs.html): a card when Arcane Intellect or your armor is missing or about to run out.
- [Buff key](buff-key.html): one key that casts what the cards ask for, then Arcane Intellect on your group.
- [Travel panel](travel-panel.html): your teleports, portals, rune counts and Hearthstone beside the Escape menu.
- [Well-Rested and Well Fed](well-rested-and-well-fed.html): a warning and a countdown before they run out.
- [Explanations](explanations.html): what each card and button means, in its tooltip.
- [Quality of life](quality-of-life.html): the minimap button, and moving and resizing frames.
- [Skins](skins.html): Frost crystal, classic Blizzard or flat, to match the rest of your UI.
