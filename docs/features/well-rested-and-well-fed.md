# Well-Rested and Well Fed

On Forever, both buffs give more experience. Chronobound tells you before one runs out, so you can sleep or eat again in time.

## What it does

- A warning in the middle of the screen, in chat and with a sound when **Well-Rested** (the Cozy Sleeping Bag buff) or **Well Fed** is about to run out, for example `Well Fed: 3 min left`.
- After the warning, a card counts down the time left.
- Well Fed also tells you when it is gone: `Well Fed is gone`.
- Optionally, a card stays up after the buff is gone ("Buff has run out"), with one rebuff warning: `Well-Rested is gone, rebuff`. The rebuff warning waits until you are out of combat and alive.

These cards are only reminders: clicking them does nothing.

## How to use it

Nothing to do: both are on by default. To change them, open `/cb`, page **Extras**. **Test** in each section plays the warning so you can hear it.

## Settings

In `/cb`, page **Extras**. The **Well-Rested** and **Well Fed** sections have the same settings:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warn | On | The warning before it runs out. Off: nothing at all for this buff. |
| Sound | On | Plays the raid warning sound with the warning and the "is gone" messages. |
| Countdown card | On | The card that counts down after the warning. |
| Keep following it after it's gone (rebuff warning) | Off | Keeps a "Buff has run out" card up after the buff is gone, with one rebuff warning. |
| Warn with time left | 5 min (Well-Rested), 3 min (Well Fed) | When the warning comes. 1 to 30 minutes. |

## Good to know

**Well Fed says when it is gone, Well-Rested doesn't.**
With **Keep following it after it's gone** on, both say so, as a rebuff warning.

**Right after you log in or change zones**, nothing is said for about 5 seconds. The game fills in your buffs a moment after loading.

**Hover over a card** to read what the buff does, when [explanations](explanations.html) are on.
