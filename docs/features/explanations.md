# Explanations

Every card and travel button has a short explanation in its tooltip: what it is, why it matters, and what to do. They are written for someone new to WoW. Once you know it all, turn them off.

## What it does

Hover over:

- **A card** (Arcane Intellect, armor, water, food, Well-Rested, Well Fed): its title and what the buff or item does. For example, the water card says that drinking conjured water brings your mana back after a fight, so you never have to buy any.
- **A card you can click**: the spell it casts, the explanation, and `Click to cast (out of combat)`.
- **A teleport, portal or the Hearthstone** on the [travel panel](travel-panel.html): what it does and what it uses. A teleport uses one Rune of Teleportation, a portal one Rune of Portals, and the Hearthstone takes you back to the inn you set it to.

The water and food cards have their own explanation when you can make a better rank: new food or water brings back more, so conjure fresh and throw the old away when you need the space.

## How to use it

Explanations are on by default. Turn them off on the **Skin** page in `/cb`, or type `/cb explain`. The same command turns them on again.

## Settings

In `/cb`, page **Skin**, section **General**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain things in tooltips | On | Plain-word help when you hover over a card or button. |

## Good to know

**With explanations off, tooltips stay short.** A clickable card still shows the spell name and `Click to cast (out of combat)`. A travel button still shows its name and either `Click to cast.` or the level you learn it at.

**Cards don't block clicks on the world.** A card only takes the mouse for its tooltip, so you can still click what is behind it. Only a card that can cast right now takes clicks.

**While the frames are unlocked**, cards show no explanation. Hovering is for dragging then.
