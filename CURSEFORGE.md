# Chronobound

**The mage helper for World of Warcraft: Forever: food and water, buffs, and every teleport and portal one Escape away.**

Chronobound keeps the mage chores in one place: it tells you when your conjured food and water run low, when Arcane Intellect or your armor is about to drop, and puts your teleports and portals right beside the game menu.

**Beta:** Chronobound is new. Spell IDs come from Classic and are checked on Forever before 0.1.0; tell us what doesn't work.

## Food and water
- **Counts your conjured food and water** and shows a card when it runs low. Click the card to conjure more, always your best rank.
- **Tells you when a better rank is learned**, so old water doesn't stay in your bags.

## Buffs
- **Arcane Intellect and your armor** (Frost Armor, Ice Armor or Mage Armor): a card when one is missing or about to run out. Click it to cast.
- Choose which armor you keep up, how early to warn, and whether to play a sound.

## Teleports and portals
- **Press Escape**: beside the game menu you get every teleport and portal you know (click to cast), the ones you'll learn later with their level, your Rune of Teleportation and Rune of Portals counts, and your Hearthstone.

## Also in it
- **Well Rested and Well Fed**: a countdown before they run out, with a sound if you like.
- **Explained**: hover over a card or button for a plain-word explanation of what it does and why (can be turned off).
- **Your layout**: every frame can be moved, snapped to a grid and scaled (`/cb unlock`).
- **Three skins**: the mage look, Blizzard and Flat.
- Loads on mages only.

## Commands
- `/cb`: open the options
- `/cb help`: list every command
- `/cb unlock`: show every frame so you can drag it into place
- `/cb lock`: lock the frames again
- `/cb reset`: put every frame back where it started
- `/cb grid [size]`: snap to a grid while moving, or set its size
- `/cb minimap`: show or hide the minimap button
- `/cb explain`: turn the plain-word explanations in tooltips on or off

## Ideas and contributions

## The Bound family
Chronobound is one of the Bound addons, one per class, built on the same core: Lightbound (paladin), Chronobound (mage), Stormbound (shaman), Packbound (hunter), Spiritbound (priest) and Felbound (warlock). They look like their class but work the same way, so if you know one, you know them all.
